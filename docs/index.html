What's CoinGecko and what's ours. Prices, market caps, trading volume, DEX pool activity and holder counts come directly from the CoinGecko API. The rotation score, quadrant (Leading / Improving / Weakening / Lagging), relative-strength and breadth figures on this page are computed by this project's own code from that data — they are not official CoinGecko classifications or financial advice. Rising trading volume on its own does not prove net capital inflow into a sector; that's why this tracker also checks DEX buy/sell flow and holder growth before calling something a rotation.
